# Privacy: Solarchik CLOCK IN (Anna App)

- The app is a static bundle served by Anna. It has no servers of its own and no analytics or trackers.
- Chats with Sol are sent to Anna's LLM through `anna.llm.complete`, and voice clips go through `anna.audio.transcribe` and `anna.audio.speak`. Anna handles them under its own privacy policy. We never receive them.
- Game progress (streak, suns, Sol's name and style) is stored locally in your browser storage inside the Anna app window.
- The microphone is used only while you hold the mic button.
